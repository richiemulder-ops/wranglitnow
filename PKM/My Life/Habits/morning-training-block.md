# Morning training block

> Sample note. It shows the shape a Habit takes, and how a Habit carries a
> Goal. Adapt it or delete it.

## The rhythm

An hour, first thing, six days a week. Rest day is Sunday and it is a real rest
day, not a light one.

## Why this one

It carries [[reach-race-weight-by-q3]]. A goal needs a carrier — a Project or a
Habit that actually moves it. This is the carrier.

Morning, because by evening there is always a reason not to.

## What makes it fail

Late nights. Travel. Anything that moves the start time past 7am — once it
slips it does not come back the same week.
