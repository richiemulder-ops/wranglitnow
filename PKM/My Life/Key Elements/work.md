# Work

> Sample note. It shows the shape a Key Element takes. Adapt it or delete it.

## What this covers

Everything I am paid to do, plus the skills and relationships that make that
possible. Projects with a client or an employer attached, professional goals,
the habits that keep the work sustainable, and the subjects I follow to stay
good at it.

Be inclusive on day one. It is easier to narrow a Key Element later than to
widen one that was drawn too tight.

## What good looks like

Work that is steady rather than frantic. I know what I am doing this week and
why it matters. Nothing important is held only in my head. When something
lands badly I find out early.

Not metrics — the shape of a healthy version of this domain.

## What I am ignoring

Side projects that earn nothing and teach nothing. Industry noise that does not
change what I do on Monday. Naming these once means I stop re-deciding them.
