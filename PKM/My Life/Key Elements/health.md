# Health

> Sample note. It shows the shape a Key Element takes. Adapt it or delete it.

## What this covers

Sleep, movement, food, and the mental side of all three. Any goal about a
number on a scale or a time on a clock anchors here. So do the habits that
carry those goals.

## What good looks like

Enough sleep that mornings are not a negotiation. Movement often enough that
it is unremarkable. Energy that does not depend on caffeine timing.

## What I am ignoring

Optimising anything. Supplements, tracking rings, protocols. This Key Element
is about consistency, not marginal gains.
